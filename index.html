Attention — Postal Warning

We have seen an uptick in mail being stolen. Please be careful!

Our advice is to ONLY MAIL CHECKS BY GOING INTO THE POST OFFICE AND PUTTING THEM IN THE INSIDE MAILBOX ONLY.

Thieves are taking mail out of home boxes when the flag is up — and they are also taking mail from the outside blue official mailboxes. They get skeleton keys made, then they "white wash" the checks for thousands of dollars.
