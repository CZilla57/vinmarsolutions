Additional Services

Extra care when you need it

Insecticide & Tick Control

We use a granular / fertilizer tick control product. It helps to reduce the population. We offer one, two, or three applications.

This is not a "cure all." You still need to be vigilant and try to avoid tick bites, or catch them quickly after they occur. (This product also helps with prevention of chinch bugs.)
